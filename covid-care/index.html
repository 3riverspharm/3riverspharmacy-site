TREATMENT, PREVENTION AND ONGOING CARE

Physician assessment and Ivermectin orders

We can arrange a health assessment with a physician and talk through Ivermectin as an option. Every order is reviewed by the physician before anything is prescribed.

  1. Fill out the order form online (secure) or on paper.
  2. The physician reviews it. We'll call you if anything needs a closer look.
